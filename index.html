Sleep and Hydration

Getting enough sleep and staying hydrated are essential for maintaining good health.

  1. Sleep for 7-9 hours per night
  2. Drink at least 8 glasses of water per day
  3. Avoid excessive caffeine and alcohol consumption
  4. Establish a regular sleep schedule
